## 📌 Executive Summary & Key Highlights

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source repositories** for building and scaling a **Corporate Wiki & Internal Knowledge Base**. These tools enable distributed teams, engineering departments, and enterprise organizations to capture, organize, audit, and retrieve institutional knowledge—covering everything from simple Markdown documentation wikis to AI-powered knowledge platforms with semantic search, automated content verification, and vector-based Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

* **Cloud SaaS Pioneers**: Notion, Atlassian Confluence, Guru, Slite, GitBook, Slab, Nuclino, Document360, Bloomfire, and Zoho Learn.
* **Self-Hosted Open-Source Champions**: AFFiNE, AppFlowy, Memos, Outline, Trilium, Wiki.js, BookStack, Etherpad, Foam, Gollum, Grist, TiddlyWiki, HedgeDoc, Dendron, SilverBullet, Documize, XWiki, and Kherad.
* **Data Sovereignty & Local-First**: Complete Docker and Kubernetes deployment options for zero-trust enterprise security, offline accessibility, Git-backed versioning, and compliance without recurring per-seat fees.

## 📊 Sector Overview & Market Dynamics

The corporate wiki and internal knowledge management software market is estimated at **$16.2B – $32.6B in 2026** (growing at ~14–17% CAGR), driven by corporate demand to organize unstructured institutional knowledge for enterprise AI and RAG search. The sector is **moderately fragmented**—anchored by dominant enterprise ecosystems (Atlassian Confluence, Notion, and Zoho), but contested by dozens of specialized documentation, API-first, and AI-native knowledge platforms competing across niche workflow and vertical requirements.

## 🛠️ Architecture & Deployment Recommendations

1. **For Enterprise & Engineering Teams Seeking Notion Parity**:
   Deploy **[Outline](https://github.com/outline/outline)** (ultra-fast, modern UX, OIDC/Slack logins) or **[AFFiNE](https://github.com/toeverything/AFFiNE)** / **[AppFlowy](https://github.com/AppFlowy-IO/AppFlowy)** for offline-first, local-first multimodal notes and boards.
2. **For Traditional Document Hierarchy & Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)**:
   Deploy **[BookStack](https://github.com/BookStackApp/BookStack)** (simple Book/Chapter/Page paradigm with low user learning curve) or **[Wiki.js](https://github.com/requarks/wiki)** (multi-editor support with LDAP/SAML integration).
3. **For Git-Backed Documentation with AI RAG & Review Workflows**:
   Pair **[Kherad](https://github.com/mohammadmaso/kherad)** or **[Gollum](https://github.com/gollum/gollum)** to maintain version control transparency without lock-in.
